# Broad cognitive improvement: evidence and open questions

## Answer

**The full broad-improvement profile is biologically constrained but scientifically open—not demonstrated, and not ruled out by the sources reviewed.** The target remains a **large gain across learning, memory, reasoning and attention in healthy people, beginning within minutes to hours, with a fully reversible enhanced state**. There is no validated numerical definition of “large” for this fictional comparison; this document does not invent one.

My confidence is:

- **High** that the reviewed evidence does not establish the full profile. The better-supported acute healthy-human drug effects are selective and modest; other approaches have missing breadth, uncertain magnitude, different timescales, or unestablished reversal.
- **High** that the cited constraints do **not** amount to an impossibility proof. They concern particular circuits, interventions, computations or resource costs. None measures a universal healthy-human ceiling or exhausts possible mechanisms.
- **Moderate** that simply intensifying familiar arousal, excitation or plasticity mechanisms is unlikely to deliver the conjunction. There is direct evidence of baseline dependence, selectivity and reciprocal costs—not just an absence of spectacular trials.
- **Low confidence about ultimate feasibility**, including whether an undiscovered lever exists and is acutely accessible. “Open” means unresolved, not likely, available, or guaranteed discoverable. I cannot assign a defensible probability or discovery date.

So yes, looking is scientifically defensible. But the search should ask **what bottleneck could be relieved across several capacities without moving costs elsewhere**, not assume a stronger stimulant or a plasticity biomarker is the answer. Rolf's suspicion that the lever might be outside established enhancer classes is compatible with the evidence; it is not evidence that such a lever exists.

## 1. What is constrained—and what is still open?

### Current human evidence sets expectations, not the biological maximum

Roberts et al. studied acute administration in healthy, non-sleep-deprived adults. Overall standardized mean differences were **0.12 for modafinil** and **0.21 for methylphenidate**; the latter's recall and sustained-attention estimates were **0.43** and **0.42**, respectively. No overall effect was found for d-amphetamine in that analysis [1]. Nicotine's meta-analysis found selected motor, attention and memory effects of **0.16–0.44**, in nonsmokers or minimally/non-deprived smokers—not merely withdrawal rescue [14]. These are genuine enhancement signals, not evidence of a simultaneous transformation across the requested domains.

Sleep-cued targeted memory reactivation has a pooled **g = 0.29, 95% CI 0.21–0.38** [17]. That strengthens previously acquired material during sleep; it does not establish a broad enhanced waking capacity or that a short stimulated sleep replaces a full night's biological functions.

It would nevertheless be wrong to say that every human estimate is small. Acute healthy-human hyperoxia studies report, among other outcomes, memory accuracy **g = 0.34**, reaction speed **0.82** and executive function **0.88**. Their review rates evidence quality low, with substantial heterogeneity and prediction intervals often crossing zero [15]. Photobiomodulation's pooled global-cognition **SMD = 0.66** and working-memory-span **1.41** are also striking; that review mixes healthy and impaired populations and heterogeneous protocols, and healthy executive-function reaction time was nonsignificant [16]. These are unresolved signals worth auditing, not confirmed broad acute states. A collection of domain effects from different studies is not joint benefit in the same people.

Effect sizes are not percentage gains in intelligence, cannot be summed into an enhancement “stack,” and do not provide a ceiling. Clinical failure also cannot establish a healthy-person impossibility theorem; clinical success cannot establish healthy enhancement. The history report makes both distinctions explicit.

### Acute adult modifiability is real, including above-control animal gains

Adamsky et al. activated astrocytes during acquisition and improved subsequent mouse recall; directly increasing neuronal activity instead impaired memory [11]. Whissell et al. activated CCK-GABA neurons in otherwise non-disease-model mice and improved several memory behaviors plus puzzle-box performance [12]. The latter was not uniform improvement on every puzzle: only the plug task was significant in the post hoc novel-obstacle comparisons. These experiments are counterexamples to “acute circuit manipulation can only rescue deficits.” They do not demonstrate a general cognitive ceiling increase or a ready human intervention: genetic targeting was a prerequisite, and retained potentiation/learning outlasted input.

Nardou et al. reopened adult-mouse **social reward learning**, with altered oxytocin-dependent plasticity and extracellular-matrix-associated expression [13]. That is evidence that a learning gate can be changed, not that all adult learning brakes share one switch. The measured windows extend over days to weeks; intoxication ending is not equivalent to the window closing. In healthy volunteers, Morgan's ketamine trial instead found acute impairment of episodic/working memory and procedural learning, with some other functions spared [22]. Post-acute learning opportunities and acute cognitive enhancement are different hypotheses.

### Constraints on a proposed lever

| Constraint | What it actually limits | Strength and unresolved gap |
|---|---|---|
| **Multiple operating optima** | Increasing one neuromodulator can help a task/person and hurt another; different regions and task components do not share a single optimum [2–3]. | Strong evidence against a one-direction “more is better” dial. Not proof that more selective, state-sensitive control cannot improve several functions. |
| **Learning needs information and correct updates** | Increasing update strength does not supply unfamiliar facts, useful feedback or the right representation. Rapid acquisition can interfere with established structure [8]. | Strong computational rationale; the size of avoidable inefficiency in a healthy human is unknown. A switch could improve use of existing knowledge, not conjure unencountered knowledge. |
| **Metabolic and physiological costs** | Signaling and maintenance require energy; additional synaptic activity has costs and disrupted supply harms function [9–10]. | Strong qualitative constraint on indiscriminate extra activity. No verified conversion from cognitive gain to global firing, ATP, heat or a safe reserve. |
| **Capacity versus allocation** | Better recall, focus or speed can result from selection, extra time, or neglect of other information—not increased joint capacity [5–7,23]. | Direct task-level tradeoffs exist. No theorem requires every improvement to have a matching loss; improving an inefficient policy could move several outcomes outward. |
| **Reversibility versus lasting change** | Turning off an input does not necessarily reverse plasticity, sleep changes, implantation or downstream effects [11,13,18]. | Evidence for stopping input is much stronger than evidence for complete state recovery. Reversible control of an already-existing process is conceptually different from rebuilding circuitry. |

**Energy is not an impossibility proof.** Howarth's model revised earlier estimates because mammalian action potentials were more efficient than assumed; its expenditure depends on cell types, firing rates and other assumptions [9]. It budgets neural processes, not intelligence. Better routing, less interference or more informative activity might improve performance without proportional growth in global firing. Whether the brain has enough accessible inefficiency for a *large, broad* gain is unknown. Neither a heat-transfer equation nor an assumed temperature boundary resolves that question.

**Evolution is not proof of a cognitive optimum either.** The reviewed literature does not show that adult cognition is globally maximized, or quantify an acutely recruitable reserve. A constraint can protect stability or serve different demands without forbidding improvement under another control policy. Conversely, calling something a protective brake is not evidence that removing it is beneficial.

## 2. Why familiar enhancer classes plateau

Here “plateau” means their limited observed gains or diminishing/negative responses, **not a proven dose-response asymptote for every class**. An efficacy average or failed trial does not itself identify the limiting mechanism.

### Catecholaminergic arousal and control: tuning, not a general multiplier

**Observed:** effects depend on baseline state and task. Both insufficient and excessive dopamine/noradrenaline can impair working memory [2–3]. Inverted-U is initially a **description of a response curve**, not an explanation by itself; Cools and D'Esposito explicitly make this distinction.

**Mechanistic evidence:** in primate prefrontal working-memory circuits, moderate D1 stimulation can suppress task-irrelevant firing and sharpen tuning; excessive stimulation suppresses relevant firing too. Noradrenaline recruits different receptor populations as levels rise: α2A-linked support of network connections versus stress-level α1-linked impairment. Intracellular cAMP/channel and phosphatidylinositol/PKC pathways supply causal circuit-level explanations for parts of the curve [3]. These mechanisms are strongest for the studied prefrontal operations, not a complete explanation of every stimulant's cognitive profile.

**Tradeoff:** stability against distraction and flexibility to update are distinct control demands; a useful optimum depends on region and situation [2]. Raising tone globally can overshoot an already effective process while improving another. That explains why baseline rescue and selected gains need not scale into broad enhancement. It does **not** establish that every rested healthy adult is already optimal, or that modafinil's entire small effect follows from this specific mechanism.

### Cholinergic enhancement: stronger selection can narrow transfer

**Observed and partially mechanistically supported:** donepezil improved motion-discrimination learning in healthy adults but also increased specificity to trained direction and location [5]. The authors connect this to more selective neural responses; their human behavioral experiment does not directly prove that cellular mediator.

The limitation is not simply “no room left.” A system selecting relevant features more effectively can learn the trained pattern better without becoming better at unfamiliar tasks. Nicotine's reproducible selected effects do not demonstrate broad reasoning or general-learning enhancement [14]. Neither source establishes a universal cholinergic dose ceiling; **specificity is shown, the whole class's ceiling is not**.

### Plasticity enhancement: learning strength is not learning quality

**Mechanistic rationale:** complementary learning systems theory explains how rapid storage can coexist with gradual integration that protects existing structure [8]. It is a model-based account supported by memory dissociations, not a measured prohibition on fast cortical learning. Global increases in update strength may overwrite useful knowledge or amplify irrelevant experience. Better credit assignment and selective protection could avoid some of that cost; their human enhancement magnitude is unestablished.

**Observed mismatch:** social-learning critical-period reopening in mice [13] and acute ketamine impairment in healthy people [22] do not join into broad beneficial performance. Persistent windows also conflict with an on-demand reversible state. Thus the plateau for proposed plasticity enhancers is often **absence of demonstrated useful transfer**, not a proven saturation mechanism. More synapses, expression change or physiological plasticity is not a behavioral effect size.

### Homeostatic compensation: a real mechanism, an unproven class-wide explanation

Turrigiano reviews calcium-dependent sensing and receptor trafficking that adjust synaptic strengths, plus other mechanisms stabilizing activity [4]. In neuronal cultures, perturbed firing can return toward baseline over many hours. Scaling can preserve relative synaptic weights while changing overall strength: it is not simply erasure of all learning.

These are **established stabilizing mechanisms in the studied preparations**. Their contribution to diminishing healthy-human enhancer benefits is a **supported possibility**, not directly established by a small acute drug effect. Chronic adaptation, an acute inverted-U and receptor tolerance are not interchangeable explanations. This source does not prove that homeostasis cancels every acute benefit, sets a fixed intellectual ceiling, or should be disabled.

### Stimulation, sleep and metabolic routes: access to a process is not broad benefit

Closed-loop temporal-cortex stimulation improved recall by targeting poor encoding states in epilepsy patients [18]. This supports timing-sensitive access to a specific process, not a fully reversible healthy-human BCI route to general cognition. Preparation and implantation are part of the intervention; no evidence here makes a bio-hybrid device the only viable route.

Sleep pharmacology supplies an unusually direct reciprocal result: greater spindle density accompanied **better verbal memory but worse perceptual learning**, with motor learning unchanged [6]. The tradeoff is observed experimentally; changing several sleep features together prevents attributing every effect exclusively to spindles. Modest TMR effects [17] likewise establish selected consolidation benefits, not all-domain capacity.

For metabolic approaches, energy costs are mechanistically real [9–10], but the literature does not establish that adequate fuel delivery is the dominant bottleneck in rested healthy brains. Hyperoxia's positive but low-quality findings [15] argue against categorical dismissal, not for a fuel-based broad-improvement mechanism. Device and metabolic plateaus are often **observed selectivity, inconsistent transfer or incomplete measurement**, not an identified universal biological ceiling.

### Speed, effort and policy can conceal the plateau

The speed–accuracy literature shows how changing decision policy changes the apparent benefit [7]. It does not prove that the frontier is immovable. In Bowman's 40-person crossover, the tested drugs lowered attained knapsack value and productivity despite increased effort on relevant measures; reaching the optimal solution was not significantly reduced [23]. Not every effort comparison increased significantly for every drug. One complex task cannot settle all everyday cognition, but it demonstrates that “worked harder” is not “thought better.”

**Overall:** familiar interventions often tune the gain, selection or timing of existing operations. They do not supply all the representations, control and information those operations need. That is a useful search diagnosis—not proof that familiar biology has no unexplored headroom.

## 3. Open directions worth pursuing

Labels apply to the **specific claim**, not to a whole field: **established** = well-supported phenomenon in the named population/preparation; **supported** = evidence-based direction with important unresolved links; **speculative** = plausible extension without direct evidence of the claimed capability. No direction below is an established broad-improvement route.

| Direction | Established / supported starting point | Speculative extension and a discriminating next question |
|---|---|---|
| **State-sensitive allocation, routing and metacontrol** | **Supported:** human planning can be modeled in terms of resource-constrained allocation, with behavioral evidence of adaptive strategy use [19]. Above-control astrocyte/interneuron experiments support looking beyond total excitation [11–12]. | **Speculative:** one acutely accessible controller improves several capacities together. Start with synthesis Route 3 and theory H1/H2/H5/H6: matched-information, matched-resource comparisons measuring retention, reasoning and attention separately. Do gains survive strong baselines without an oracle, extra replay or sacrificed old knowledge? A successful model demonstrates a computation, not human reserve or biological access. |
| **Poorly characterized adult cognition biology** | **Established:** Unknome identifies understudied conserved proteins and experimental screens find functions [20]; intelligence GWAS identifies associations and biological enrichments [21]. **Supported:** combine these with locus alternatives, adult cell context and perturbation evidence, as dry-lab Projects 1/3/4 and synthesis Route 2 propose. | **Speculative:** a candidate controls acute broad cognition. Lifelong associations can reflect development and pleiotropy; a gene assignment does not establish beneficial intervention direction. The useful output is a causal hypothesis with competing genes and disconfirming observations—not a molecule, cellular blueprint or safety certificate. |
| **Selective learning gates with interference protection** | **Supported:** mouse critical-period reopening and metaplastic/ECM findings identify a controllable learning gate [13]. A shared causal state program is **not established** by the existing Route 1 experiment. | **Speculative:** acutely controlled learning susceptibility generalizes across domains without acute impairment or lasting unwanted susceptibility. First resolve independent biological evidence and nuisance explanations; do not optimize the inconclusive predictor into a discovery. Subsequent supervised research would need acquisition slopes, old-knowledge protection and new-material tests after the window ends. Even success would not automatically establish immediate reasoning/attention gain. |
| **Precision, closed-loop network control** | **Supported:** state-dependent recall improvement in implanted patients [18], plus the selective healthy noninvasive findings mapped in the non-drug report. The human result is real causal leverage, not general enhancement. | **Speculative:** controllable network coordination improves joint healthy performance rather than just weak encoding. Synthesis Route 6 can evaluate public timing data and useful recall per elapsed time; waiting for “good states” may erase benefit. Device-off is not proof of full physiological recovery, and invasive systems are not fully reversible whole interventions. |
| **Independent audit of larger acute human signals** | **Supported:** hyperoxia and photobiomodulation have published substantial estimates, with the limitations above [15–16]. | **Speculative:** a reproducible full-profile effect is concealed by fragmented measurements. Start with synthesis Route 5: separate healthy populations, acute versus repeated protocols, dependent endpoints and speed/accuracy. Missing numerical inputs remain unavailable, not zero effects. An audit can justify a supervised confirmatory study; it cannot establish safety or broad state reversal. |
| **Selective consolidation and memory access** | **Established:** sleep TMR improves selected memory outcomes [17]. **Supported:** the theory report's retrieval/indexing and interference hypotheses explain why usable memory may differ from storage capacity. | **Speculative:** a shared access or consolidation lever has large cross-domain consequences. Compare retrieval errors, useful transfer and competing memories, not just cued recall. This is a component-level search/control case; stronger memories alone do not meet the full broad-improvement profile or justify sleep compression. |

**Where I would start:** use the merged synthesis's bounded routes, but update its first-route recommendation with the actual **inconclusive Route 1 verdict**. Do not promote its expression ranks into enhancer candidates. For unfamiliar biology, the understudied-gene dossier is a concrete public-data entry; for a possible acute reversible mechanism, fixed-resource control-policy comparisons ask the sharper feasibility question. These are complementary hypothesis-generating starts, not forecasts of efficacy. Keep larger human signals visible for audit rather than assuming only old stimulants deserve investigation.

Laptop work can reject an explanation, prioritize a locus, or establish sufficiency in a model. It cannot measure exact human bandwidth, identify every hidden bottleneck, or certify a safe reversible enhancer. Moving from a lead to the requested biological state requires causal experimental and supervised human evidence; no administration, design, synthesis, doses or self-experimentation are proposed here.

## 4. What would change the answer?

The unknown is the **conjunction of headroom and acute biological access**. A convincing advance would independently reproduce substantial useful gains across unfamiliar learning, later memory, reasoning and attention in the **same rested healthy population**, beyond practice and expectancy, with comparable time/opportunity and reciprocal losses made visible. It would measure onset and subsequent recovery of capacity on **new material**, plus unwanted physiological/functional persistence. Retaining knowledge learned while enhanced is compatible with reversal of the enhanced state; pharmacological clearance or a nonsignificant follow-up alone does not establish complete reversal. Detailed measurement choices are in the history report.

A failed plasticity trial, negative shortlist, or unsuccessful controller would narrow that branch, not rule out all broad-improvement biology. A general impossibility claim would need a genuinely general bound linked to behavioral capacity and all admissible mechanisms; none of these sources supplies one. There is no honest numerical threshold or single decisive current experiment that can replace that missing argument.

**Bottom line:** current classes reveal why blunt enhancement stalls, and real exceptions justify searching. The literature can constrain candidate stories and identify tractable questions. It cannot currently tell Rolf that the full effect must exist—or that biology forbids it.
