# Verdict: association inventory, unresolved causal-gene shortlist

**No poorly understood causal cognition gene is established.** This is the [plan's](PLAN.md) unresolved-neighbouring-gene failure mode, not a null for undiscovered cognition biology. Follow-up covers three genomically prioritized regions; it does not exhaust the 38 Pharos-Tdark associations elsewhere in the inventory.

## Reproduced facts

- The original Lee CP file has 10,098,325 SNPs and 13,714 at the source's genome-wide threshold. Catalog identifies 257,841 European-ancestry participants; Lee's methods specify UKB plus COGENT. This is **direct CP**, not educational attainment or MTAG.
- The existing common/rare intersection remains empty and the KDM5B/NACC1 educational-attainment proxy remains separate. Those existing files are unchanged, not rerun as new validation.
- Coordinates, source build documentation, gene IDs and alleles can be reconciled with GENCODE v19 and public EUR reference data for the pilot. All three region leads have LD coverage. Missing/unoriented variants stay missing.

## New analyses of existing public data

- A conservative SNP-min union-bound procedure yields **406 gene-body associations among 18,913 tested genes**. These are new analysis outputs from an old GWAS, **not 406 novel discoveries or causal genes**. Knownness did not select evidence or rank genes.
- The strongest three physical regions contain **199 alternative genes**. In the leading region, Tdark **CDHR4** shares highly correlated evidence with competitors (body-lead r²=0.972); **C3orf62** has weaker, prior-sensitive shared-signal evidence; **CIMIP7** lacks oriented lead-LD and aligned DLPFC QTL coverage. Low knownness cannot resolve any of them.
- **RNF123** is a stronger adult-expression/shared-signal hypothesis, but is Tbio, not Tdark. Primary single-signal H4=0.924; documented assumptions/windows give 0.494 to 0.992. This warrants a falsifiable hypothesis about the shared regulatory signal, not exclusive gene assignment or expression mediation. Competing genes and multiple signals remain.
- **SLC39A8 rs13107325 A391T** supplies a coding hypothesis as a useful contrast, not unexplained biology or a manipulation direction. **POU3F2** body association does not explain R002's lead: its body-lead r²≈0.000026 exposes the danger of treating merged regions as single signals.
- Matched length/density/expression comparisons do not establish an enrichment of poorly understood genes. Annotation missingness and the conservative test's power dependence remain visible.

## Open causal questions and unavailable scope

A credible assignment needs signal-specific comparisons that discriminate neighbours, not the lowest publication count. Single-causal-variant coloc, changing priors or narrowing windows does not replace multiple-signal conditioning; different genes have different observed QTL subsets. RNF123's shared signal could be co-regulation or pleiotropy rather than mediation. Adult RNA/QTLs do not establish adult causal timing; development, systemic physiology, health and indirect genetic effects remain alternatives.

Only GTEx frontal-cortex QTLs were analyzed; other tissues/cell types, conditional fine-mapping and a genome-wide alternative-annotation rerun remain unestablished. Neuronal perturbation coverage is **unavailable per pilot gene**: source metadata and guide sequences are public, but target/phenotype XLSX requests return HTML challenges. The screen's survival/transcription readouts and kinase/druggable-library bias cannot substitute for cognition. No K562 phenotype was promoted.

Specific disconfirming observations and dependencies are recorded per association in `ranked-evidence.tsv`. Savage mappings/MAGMA and Genebass are **overlapping evidence**, not independent replication; HPA consensus and GTEx also reuse expression data. Missing data are not biological negatives.

**The dossier establishes neither a beneficial manipulation direction, adult-specific causality nor intervention efficacy or safety.** It is not an intervention target list. This bounded resolution failure is not a verdict on all public genetics.
